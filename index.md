Welcome to the [Continuous DEMs](https://continuous-dems.github.io) community compass!

**A community compass is a place for community members, new and old, to align on objectives,
practices, and happenings.**

Our Community Compass contains community practices, policies, and resources to help everyone align and contribute to the Continuous DEMs ecosystem. It also has helpful information for community members, like shared infrastructure and accounts, resources to learn how to contribute, etc.

We build and maintain an ecosystem of open-source projects dedicated to processing Digital Elevation Models (DEMs). Our vision is to cultivate a thriving open-source community around high-accuracy elevation modeling. Whether you are looking for the right tool for your DEM pipeline or want to contribute to our ecosystem, you are in the right place.

We use [GitHub issues in continuous-dems/community-compass](https://github.com/continuous-dems/community-compass/issues) to discuss specific, actionable things related to the community and compass.
